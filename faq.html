Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions.

Orders & Delivery
How do I track my order? ⌃
Go to Help → Track Order and enter your Order ID or registered email. You'll see real-time status and estimated delivery time.
How long does delivery take? ⌃
Most orders are delivered within 1–3 business days in metros and 3–5 days in other cities. Express delivery is available at checkout for select pin codes.
Can I change my delivery address after placing an order? ⌃
You can change the address within 1 hour of placing the order. After that, contact our support team immediately — we'll do our best to update it before dispatch.
What if I'm not available at the time of delivery? ⌃
Our delivery partner will attempt delivery 3 times. You can also reschedule via the tracking link sent to your email/SMS. After 3 failed attempts, the order is returned to us.
Payments
What payment methods do you accept? ⌃
We accept UPI, credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex), net banking, EMI, wallets (Paytm, PhonePe), and Cash on Delivery.
Is it safe to save my card on RemZe? ⌃
Yes. RemZe uses bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption. We are fully PCI-DSS compliant and never store your full card details on our servers.
My payment failed but money was deducted. What do I do? ⌃
This is usually a temporary bank hold. Deducted amounts are automatically reversed within 5–7 business days. If not, contact us with your transaction ID and we'll escalate immediately.
Returns & Refunds
What is the return window? ⌃
Most products have a 10-day return window from the date of delivery. Electronics and mobiles have a 7-day window. See our Returns page for full details.
When will I get my refund? ⌃
Once your return is received and inspected, the refund is processed within 2 business days. It reflects in your account within 5–7 business days depending on your bank.
I received a damaged/wrong product. What should I do? ⌃
Please raise a complaint within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item. We'll arrange an immediate pickup and replacement or full refund at no charge.
Account
How do I create a RemZe account? ⌃
Click Login → Sign Up on the top-right of any page. You can sign up with your email or mobile number. Signing up is free and takes under a minute.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it? ⌃
Click Login → Forgot Password, enter your registered email or phone, and follow the link/OTP sent to you.
