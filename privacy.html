1. Information We Collect

When you use RemZe, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, mobile number, and password.
  • Order & Transaction Data: Purchase history, payment method details (tokenised), billing and delivery addresses.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, clicks, search queries, and time spent on the platform.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we collect approximate or precise location to improve delivery estimates and show relevant results.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process orders, payments, and deliveries.
  • Personalise your shopping experience and show relevant recommendations.
  • Send transactional communications (order confirmations, shipping updates).
  • Send promotional offers and newsletters (you can opt out at any time).
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Improve our platform, products, and customer support.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in the following cases:

  • Sellers & Delivery Partners: To fulfil your orders (name, address, phone number only).
  • Payment Processors: Tokenised payment data shared with RBI-compliant payment gateways.
  • Analytics & Marketing Tools: Aggregated, anonymised data only.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government authority.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember your cart, analyse site usage, and show relevant ads. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some platform features.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.
  • Withdraw consent for location or notification access.

To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@remze.in.

6. Data Security

We use 256-bit SSL encryption, secure servers, and strict access controls to protect your data. We are PCI-DSS compliant for all payment data handling. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights, we will notify you within 72 hours.

7. Children's Privacy

RemZe is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
