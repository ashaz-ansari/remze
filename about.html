Our Story

RemZe was founded in 2022 by a small team of e-commerce enthusiasts who believed that online shopping could be simpler, faster, and more affordable. Starting with just a handful of categories and a handful of sellers, we grew rapidly by putting customers first.

Today, RemZe is one of India's fastest-growing online marketplaces, serving millions of customers across fashion, electronics, home goods, and more. We partner with thousands of trusted sellers to bring you genuine products with price-match guarantees.

Our platform is built on cutting-edge technology to make discovery, purchase, and delivery as seamless as possible — whether you're shopping from a metro city or a small town.
