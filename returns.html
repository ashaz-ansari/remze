Return Policy at a Glance

Returns accepted within 10 days of delivery for most products.
Items must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging with all tags.
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment source.
For damaged or wrong items, raise a request within 48 hours of delivery.
Exchange option available for fashion items (size/colour) subject to stock availability.
